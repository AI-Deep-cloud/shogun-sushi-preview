Shogun Sushi

A table for every appetite.

Shogun Sushi is a sushi and Japanese-inspired restaurant located at 11750 1st Avenue in Saint-Georges, in the Beauce region of Quebec. It offers an all-you-can-eat formula in the dining room, as well as a full menu available for pickup and delivery.

On the menu: special rolls with evocative names — Alaska, Black Dragon, Le Québécois, Honey Bomb — sushi and sashimi, combos from 17 to 104 pieces, poke bowls and hot dishes such as chicken teriyaki, General Tao chicken and fried udon.

The name

将軍, shōgun

In Japan, the title of shōgun was held by the great military leaders who ruled the country for nearly seven centuries, from the late 12th century until 1868.
