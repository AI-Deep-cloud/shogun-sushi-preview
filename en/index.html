Welcome to Shogun

A generous Japanese table on 1st Avenue.

Guests come to Shogun Sushi for the all-you-can-eat experience, a platter to share with friends or a comforting hot dish. The menu runs from the great classics — California, Philadelphia, Dynamite — to special rolls such as the Alaska, the Golden Dragon and Le Québécois.

Staying in? The menu is also available online for pickup or delivery.

  • All you can eat

    A dine-in formula to taste a bit of everything as the meal goes on.

  • More than 140 choices

    Rolls, sushi, sashimi, combos, poke bowls and hot dishes.

  • Dine in or take out

    Dining room, terrace, online ordering and delivery.

  • Reservations

    Book your table by phone at 418-221-7300.
